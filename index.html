What is SG Baddy?

SG Baddy is a mobile app (iOS and Android) for Singapore's social badminton community. Hosts list games with a skill-level range and a per-slot price; players at that level discover the games, join, and pay securely in the app. After each game, players anonymously rate each other, which keeps everyone's declared level accurate over time.

The app is currently in closed testing ahead of its public launch.
