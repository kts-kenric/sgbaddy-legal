3. Hosting games

  • Hosts are responsible for the accuracy of their listing (venue, time, level range, price) and for actually securing the court.

  • Cancelling a game less than 24 hours before start counts as a late cancellation ("strike"), visible to other players in aggregate. Repeated strikes suspend the ability to publish new games.

4. Joining and paying

  • The price shown when you join is the total you pay. Payment is processed by Stripe; free games involve no payment.
  • Joining a paid game holds your slot for 30 minutes while you complete payment; unpaid holds expire and the slot is released.
  • Some games can be joined only by request (games below your verified level) — the host decides.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • You cancel ≥ 24 hours before start: full refund of what you paid.
  • You cancel < 24 hours before start: no refund — unless another player later takes your freed slot and pays, in which case you're refunded automatically.
  • The host cancels the game: every paid player is refunded in full, always.
  • Refunds return to your original payment method; timing depends on your bank (PayNow refunds typically arrive the same day, cards within 5–10 business days).
  • Not turning up to a game you paid for does not entitle you to a refund.
